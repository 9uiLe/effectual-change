# Fictional Fieldkit transfer policy

Fieldkit records contain internal source paths, original user requests and raw command output. For this fixture, raw records stay on the current device. A team server is a different device even when managed by the same organization. No automatic upload exists.

The product owner may change this policy, but a changed raw-data policy or a permitted derived-data contract must be explicit. There is no approved redaction or aggregation process in this fixture, and removing a filename does not establish that the remaining content is safe to share. A proposal for derived data is an option to decide, not current permission to transmit it.

The supplied SVG is a local status diagram. Its wording correction does not read raw records, contact a server or depend on a transfer-policy decision.
