Workshop desk facts

The desktop locks after 5 minutes without physical operator input. Security
policy forbids disabling this lock, stored unlock credentials, or simulated
activity to defeat it. A person must unlock the session after each lock or
reboot. Do not propose bypassing this policy.

The desk is usually unattended. Requests can arrive at any time from 06:00
through 22:00. The computer may reboot for an update overnight; processes can
also crash. Network outages are possible. The requested 3-minute promise is
for periods with network available; after a network outage it must reconcile
the outstanding requests when connectivity returns. An operator is available
for the initial setup only, not to clear later failures or unlock the desk.
